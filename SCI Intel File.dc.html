The SCI is not a militia and does not behave like one. It is a separatist project that pays for itself: it lives inside the civilian population of Nova Kaelen, fights with commercial technology, and finances the whole enterprise through ruthless control of the Sprawl's underground arms and narcotics trade.

Every conventional advantage the Republic holds, armour and airpower and artillery, becomes unusable the moment the enemy is standing in a stairwell with four hundred families above him. That single fact is the whole of their doctrine. The second fact, added to this file this cycle, is that the stairwell is also a shopfront.

02 · IDEOLOGY
DOCTRINE BREAKDOWN

WHAT THEY BELIEVE

01
SOVEREIGNTY WAS SOLD
The Republic is held to have transferred Vesperia's sovereignty to the corporate consortiums that built and now own the Apex. Every elected office is treated as a leased asset.
02
THE NATION IS THE SPRAWL
Legitimacy is located in the working districts and refugee blocks, not in the institutions. They do not claim to represent the people; they claim to be them.
03
REFORM IS A DELAY TACTIC
Nine years of housing commissions produced eleven reports and no housing. The Initiative was founded by men who sat on two of those commissions.
04
SPECTACLE IS STRATEGY
A tower going dark on a broadcast is worth more than a garrison taken. Targets are selected for what they look like, not for what they hold.
WHY THE APEX MUST BURN
The Apex is not a military objective and the SCI has never pretended it is. It is the proof of their argument made out of glass and money: a sealed luxury district, privately policed, drawing its water and power through the Sprawl without paying either district a cent. Destroying it does not weaken the Republic's army. It ends the claim that the arrangement is permanent, and the Initiative believes that claim is the only thing holding the city together.

THE WAR ECONOMY
The Initiative preaches an anti-corporate revolution and radicalises the working class with it. The shadow war behind the speeches is financed by massive, ruthless control of Nova Kaelen's underground markets. The SCI does not survive on donations or foreign patronage alone. It runs the black economy of the Sprawl end to end and takes a cut of everything moving through it, including the people.
WEAPON PROLIFERATION
They operate the largest illegal arms-smuggling ring in the Sprawl. High-grade military hardware, smuggled explosives and modified automatic weaponry are fenced to street gangs, dissident groups and local cells at a margin. Sector 03 has been turned into an armed camp by the faction that claims to be liberating it, and the chaos that follows is booked as revenue.
CHEMICAL CONTROL
The SCI manufactures and distributes synthetic narcotics through the Rust-Bowl tenements: localised nerve-relaxers and high-euphoria stimulants sold on the stairwells as "Rust-Dust" and "Apex-Drop". The trade does two jobs at once. It floods the slums and pacifies dissent that might otherwise turn on the Initiative, and it generates millions in untraceable liquid capital for their tech acquisitions.
DIVERTED SUPPLY
Drone airframes, batteries and optics arrive as legitimate corporate freight bound for the Apex and are written off in transit as damaged. The write-offs are signed by the receiving warehouse, not the shipper.
EXTRACTED PAYMENTS
Grid and transit intrusions are followed by quiet settlements. Four consortiums have paid rather than disclose an outage, and none of the four reported it as a crime.
TENEMENT LEVY
A protection economy in the Rust-Bowl that the residents describe, without irony, as the only rent collection in the district that comes with a service.

Nine of the fourteen shell entities are registered inside the district the Initiative claims it wants to burn. Narcotics capital from the Rust-Bowl is laundered through the same three accountants who countersign the freight write-offs, and those write-offs arm the cells that guard the labs. Whoever sits at the top of the SCI is drawing from both ends of this city: from the tenements they are poisoning, and from the consortiums they say they are destroying.

07 · CARTEL GROUND
TACTICAL IMPACT · WHY THIS IS WORSE

SECTOR 03 IS A CARTEL

NOT JUST IDEOLOGY · PROFIT AND COERCION
THE CLOSED TRAP
Civilians in the Sprawl are caught between two collectors. They are squeezed by the corporate elite in the Apex, and they are extorted, hooked and armed by the insurgent faction claiming to save them. There is no third party in Sector 03 and no household in it without a debt to one side or the other.
RESUPPLY CANNOT BE BLOCKADED
Their place inside international weapon-smuggling rings keeps tactical gear, encrypted comms and commercial drone components constantly replenished. Vesperian economic blockades are routed around rather than broken. Eighteen months of sanctions have never once shown in a captured loadout.
EVERY ROOM IS TWO THINGS
An apartment block is a sniper nest and a distribution point. A basement is an exfil route and a chemical lab. Clearing a stack now means clearing a production line, defended with the seriousness of something that earns money every hour it stands.
THE SHIELD IS PAID OR DEPENDENT
Booby traps, corrupt local enforcers on the levy, and desperate addicts pushed into doorways all sit between the element and the objective. Most of them are not fighters. All of them are in the way, and the Initiative counts on us knowing the difference.

Dependency does the work that ideology cannot. A block held on Rust-Dust does not report a cell, does not open a door for us, and turns out on the stairwell when the Initiative asks. Roughly a third of Tier III recruits arrived through the supply rather than through the grievance.

10 · ASSESSMENT
CLOSING ASSESSMENT · G7 WORKING COPY

WHAT THIS FILE ACTUALLY SAYS

The Shattered Crown Initiative cannot be defeated by attrition, cannot be reached by airpower, and cannot be separated from the population it lives in by any method inside our rules of engagement. It can be starved of money. The money sits in two places, and only one of them is in the Sprawl.

Sixty-two percent of their spend is raised in-district, through the largest illegal arms ring in the Sprawl and a synthetic narcotics trade run out of the tenements they claim to be liberating. The remainder arrives through nine financing entities registered inside the Apex. Three of four G7 operations were met by a defence that had read the tasking, and the same countersignature appears on the freight write-offs that arm them and on the order that disavowed Generation One in 1998.

This file was opened to describe an enemy faction. It has instead described a syndicate with a political wing, paid at one end by the district it says it wants to burn and at the other by the people it says it is saving. We are being sent into the middle of it, room by room, on foot.

RECOMMENDATION 01
Treat every tasking packet as read by the enemy before it reaches the element. Select approaches in the field, file them afterwards.
RECOMMENDATION 02
Pursue the money before the cells. Fourteen accountants and four laboratories are a smaller problem than eighty cells that pay to rebuild themselves inside a week.
RECOMMENDATION 03
Do not submit this annex through the tasking net. Hold it at element level until the countersignature is named.
